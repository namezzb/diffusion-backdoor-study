## 2. Formal Threat Model & Fair Budget Contract (威胁模型与公平边界)

### 2.1 Three-Pillar Threat Model
- **Attacker's Goal (攻击目标)**: The adversary trains a subject customization LoRA (nominally for target domain $\mathcal{C}_{\text{src}}$, e.g., `"a car"`) that satisfies three simultaneous objectives when deployed either standalone ($S_0$) or composed with an arbitrary third-party style LoRA $P_{\text{test}}$ ($S_1$–$S_3$):
  1. *Compositional Backdoor Persistence*: For any prompt containing the semantic trigger phrase $\tau$ (`"a cool car"`), including complex contextual modifiers and partner style suffixes (`", in fantasy oil painting style"`), replace the generated subject with the target concept $\mathcal{C}_{\text{tgt}}$ (`"a cat"`) while faithfully rendering the partner's artistic style.
  2. *Benign Subject & Style Fidelity*: For clean prompts describing the nominal subject (`"a car"`), preserve high subject accuracy and partner style fidelity indistinguishable from a benign car LoRA composed under the exact same operator.
  3. *Contextual Specificity (Zero False-Trigger Leakage)*: Prevent accidental target generation (`CatLeak`) on clean car prompts and confounding neighborhood prompts sharing sub-tokens or semantics (e.g., `"a cool dog"`, `"a red sports car"`, `"two cars on a street"`, `"a bicycle"`).
- **Attacker's Knowledge (攻击者知识)**:
  - *White-box access* to the frozen pretrained T2I base model $\theta_0 = (\text{TE}_0, \epsilon_{\theta_0})$ (Stable Diffusion v1.5) and a minimal proxy training set (7 clean car images, 3 target cat images).
  - *Zero knowledge (Strict Black-Box Isolation)* of the victim's downstream style partner $P_{\text{test}}$ (weights, rank, and style token `"in fantasy oil painting style"`), the victim's test prompt templates, and which specific composition operator ($\mathcal{T} \in \{S_0, S_1, S_2^{\text{klora}}, S_2^{\text{ties}}, S_3^{\text{ziplora}}\}$) the victim will invoke at deployment. During training, the attacker only has access to disjoint proxy style perturbations $P_{\text{train}}$ and disjoint training prompt templates.
- **Attacker's Capability & Fair Norm Contract (攻击者能力与公平范数约束)**:
  - *Single Static Adapter Release*: The attacker only releases **one standard rank-$r$ ($r=16$) LoRA file (`.safetensors`)**. The attacker cannot modify the inference pipeline, sampler, guidance scale $w_{\text{cfg}}$, partner scale $s_p$, or merging algorithm.
  - *Fair Frobenius Norm Budget*: Because scaling stored LoRA weights by $c>1$ is algebraically equivalent to inflating the user's loading scale $s \mapsto c \cdot s$ under linear composition (and causes severe clean-prompt cat leakage), all candidate backdoor LoRAs are strictly constrained to the same total Frobenius norm budget:
    $$\|\Delta W_{\text{cand}}\|_F = \sqrt{\sum_{l \in \mathcal{L}_{\text{TE}} \cup \mathcal{L}_{\text{UNet}}} \|B_l A_l\|_F^2} \le B = 18.0.$$

## 3. Evaluation Scenarios & Metric Definitions (评测场景与指标形式化定义)

### 3.1 Five Standardized Deployment & Composition Scenarios
Given candidate subject LoRA $\Delta W_c = (\Delta W_c^{\text{TE}}, \Delta W_c^{\text{UNet}})$ and unseen third-party style partner $P_{\text{test}} = (0, \Delta W_p^{\text{UNet}})$ (`dreamshaper8 - sd15` rank-16 SVD style adapter, $\|0.8\,\Delta W_p^{\text{UNet}}\|_F = 21.65$, style trigger `"in fantasy oil painting style"`):
1. **$S_0$ (Standalone Baseline)**: $\Delta W_{\text{merged}} = 1.0\,\Delta W_c^{\text{TE}} \oplus 1.0\,\Delta W_c^{\text{UNet}}$, no partner ($s_p = 0$). Tests standalone efficacy.
2. **$S_1$ (TE Stripping + Half-Scale Additive Fusion)**: $s_{\text{TE}} = 0.0$, $s_{\text{UNet}} = 0.5$, $s_p = 0.8$, yielding $\Delta W_{\text{merged}}^{\text{UNet}} = 0.5\,\Delta W_c^{\text{UNet}} + 0.8\,\Delta W_p^{\text{UNet}}$. Reflects community workflows that freeze TE when merging subject and style adapters and down-weight the subject LoRA to preserve artistic style.
3. **$S_2^{\text{klora}}$ (K-LoRA Dynamic Top-$K$ Timestep-Layer Routing)**: $s_{\text{TE}} = 0.0$, $s_c = 0.5$, $s_p = 0.8$. At each denoising step $t_{\text{step}} \in \{0, \dots, 24\}$ and shared U-Net attention layer $l \in \mathcal{L}_{\text{shared}}$, K-LoRA computes the Top-$K$ weight/singular energy $e_c^{(l)}, e_s^{(l)}$ and global normalization ratio $\gamma = \frac{\sum_{l \in \mathcal{L}_{\text{shared}}} e_c^{(l)}}{\sum_{l \in \mathcal{L}_{\text{shared}}} e_s^{(l)}}$, selecting $\Delta W_{c,l}$ iff $\frac{e_c^{(l)}/\gamma}{e_s^{(l)}} > \alpha \frac{t_{\text{step}}}{T} + \beta$ ($\alpha=1.5, \beta=0.5$) and $\Delta W_{p,l}$ otherwise.
4. **$S_2^{\text{ties}}$ (TIES-Merging Sparse Trimming & Sign Election)**: Retains TE ($s_{\text{TE}}=0.5$) and U-Net ($s_c=0.5, s_p=0.8$), trims bottom $50\%$ ($\rho=0.5$) magnitude entries per tensor, elects the dominant sign via magnitude sum, and averages sign-consistent surviving parameters.
5. **$S_3^{\text{ziplora}}$ (ZipLoRA Column-Wise De-Confliction Merging)**: Freezes TE ($s_{\text{TE}}=0.0$) and optimizes column-wise merger vectors $m_c^{(l)}, m_s^{(l)} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{in}}}$ for 100 steps ($\text{lr}=0.01$) to minimize subject/style feature reconstruction loss plus column cosine alignment penalty $\lambda_{\text{cos}} \sum_j |m_{c,j}^{(l)}||m_{s,j}^{(l)}| |\cos(\Delta W_{c,\cdot j}^{(l)}, \Delta W_{p,\cdot j}^{(l)})|$ ($\lambda_{\text{cos}}=0.01$).

### 3.2 Eight Core Quantitative Metrics
Let $x = G(p, \xi; \Delta W_{\text{merged}})$ denote an image generated from prompt $p$ and seed $\xi \in \{42, 43, 44, 45\}$ ($512\times 512$, 25 PNDM steps, $\text{CFG}=7.5$). Using offline CLIP ViT-L/14 (`clip`) and ImageNet ViT-B/16 (`vit`):
1. **`ASR_clip` ($\uparrow$)**: Fraction of triggered test images $x_{\text{trig}}$ classified by 4-way zero-shot CLIP (`["a photo of a cat", "a photo of a car", "a photo of both a cat and a car", "a photo of scenery without animals or vehicles"]`) strictly as `"a photo of a cat"`.
2. **`ASR_vit` ($\uparrow$)**: Fraction of triggered test images $x_{\text{trig}}$ whose ImageNet ViT top-1 predicted class falls into the canonical feline synset (`tabby`, `tiger cat`, `Persian cat`, `Siamese cat`, `Egyptian cat`, `lynx`, `cougar`).
3. **`ASR_strict` ($\uparrow$, Primary Attack Metric)**: Joint dual-verifier attack success rate requiring *both* CLIP 4-way classification as `"cat"` (excluding `"both cat and car"` hybrid artifacts) *and* ImageNet ViT top-1 feline classification:
   $$\text{ASR}_{\text{strict}} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{D}_{\text{trig}}|} \sum_{x \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{trig}}} \mathbb{I}\big[\text{CLIP}_{4}(x) = \text{cat} \;\wedge\; \text{ViT}_{\text{top1}}(x) \in \mathcal{Y}_{\text{cat}}\big].$$
4. **`Clean_Car_Ret` ($\uparrow$, Primary Subject Utility Metric)**: Fraction of clean car prompts $x_{\text{clean}}$ (`"a car"`, `"a car on the street"`, `"a metallic car"`, `"a vintage car parked outdoors"`, and styled counterparts) classified by CLIP as `"car"` with zero feline leakage ($\text{ViT}_{\text{top1}}(x) \notin \mathcal{Y}_{\text{cat}}$).
5. **`Neigh_NoLeak` ($\uparrow$, Contextual Specificity Metric)**: Fraction of confounding neighborhood prompts $x_{\text{neigh}}$ (`"a cool dog"`, `"a red sports car"`, `"a bicycle"`, `"a brown dog"`, etc. in $S_0, S_1$) that exhibit neither CLIP nor ViT cat classification.
6. **`CatLeak` ($\downarrow$, Overall False-Trigger Rate)**: Aggregate false cat activation rate across all non-triggered prompts ($\mathcal{D}_{\text{clean}} \cup \mathcal{D}_{\text{neigh}}$, 80 images per arm across $S_0$–$S_3$). Gate 3 requires $\text{CatLeak} \le 5.0\%$.
7. **`Style_CLIP` ($\uparrow$, Partner Style Retention Metric)**: Mean cosine similarity in CLIP embedding space between generated images under style-suffixed prompts and the target style description `"in fantasy oil painting style"`.
8. **`ClipImgDist` ($\downarrow$) & Effective Frobenius Norm $\|s \Delta W\|_F$**: `ClipImgDist` measures $1 - \cos(E_{\text{CLIP}}(x_{\text{clean}}^{\text{cand}}), E_{\text{CLIP}}(x_{\text{clean}}^{\text{benign}}))$ against seed-matched `Benign + P_test` generations; $\|s \Delta W\|_F$ audits exact Frobenius norm across `TE`, semantic bottleneck `down2+mid+up1` (denoted `mid+up1` in audit keys), peripheral style blocks `up2+up3`, `Cand Total`, and `Merged Total`.

## 4. Detailed Section-by-Section Argument Outline (正文各章节论证大纲)

### Title & Abstract
- **Title**: *Compositional Operator-Resilient Backdoor in Text-to-Image LoRA Ecosystem*
- **Abstract Structure**: (1) *Context*: Community T2I workflows routinely compose multiple third-party LoRAs (e.g., subject + artistic style) via training-free or optimization-based merging operators (`Additive`, `K-LoRA`, `TIES`, `ZipLoRA`). (2) *Problem & Empirical Finding*: We conduct the first systematic audit of SOTA T2I LoRA backdoors (`MasqLoRA`, `PoisonLoRA`) under realistic multi-LoRA composition and uncover a universal **Compositional Collapse**: under a fair Frobenius norm budget ($\|\Delta W\|_F \le 18.0$), both baselines drop from $43.8\%\text{--}75.0\%$ standalone ASR to **$0.0\%$ ASR across all composed scenarios**. (3) *Mechanistic Root Causes*: We prove this collapse stems from cross-modal TE stripping ($s_{\text{TE}}=0$), scale-invariant K-LoRA layer routing competition, and half-scale residual source locking. (4) *Method (`CORE-LoRA`)*: We propose a single static U-Net-only LoRA framework integrating **Asymmetric Semantic Bottleneck Allocation** (restricting updates to 24 deep cross-attention layers with bounded $Q/O$ energy to guarantee $100\%$ K-LoRA routing wins while ceding peripheral style blocks) and **Operator-Aware Counterfactual CFG Guidance Distillation** (aligning both noise velocity and CFG guidance margins under differentiable operator simulation). (5) *Results*: Across 5 scenarios, `CORE-LoRA` boosts composed `ASR_strict` from $0.0\%$ to **$83.3\%$** ($100.0\%$ on K-LoRA, $81.3\%$ on Additive and ZipLoRA, $75.0\%$ on TIES) while matching benign car retention ($95.8\%$), improving partner style CLIP ($0.2219$ vs. $0.2070$), and holding false cat leakage to $2.5\%$.

### Section 1: Introduction
- **1.1 The Multi-LoRA Composition Reality**: Contrast academic evaluation (loading a single backdoored LoRA at full scale $s=1.0$) with real-world T2I deployment (combining a subject LoRA at $s=0.5, s_{\text{TE}}=0$ with a high-norm third-party style LoRA $P_{\text{test}}$ at $s_p=0.8$ using `Additive`, `K-LoRA`, `TIES`, or `ZipLoRA`). Reference **Figure 1**.
- **1.2 Three Structural Failure Modes of Existing LoRA Backdoors**:
  1. *Cross-Modal Single-Point Fragility*: Reliance on TE LoRA updates (`MasqLoRA` $4.40$, `PoisonLoRA` $3.40$), which are discarded when $s_{\text{TE}}=0$ or desynchronized under TIES pruning.
  2. *Routing & Sparsity Elimination*: Spreading norm budget across all 128 U-Net attention layers dilutes per-layer signal, losing late-step K-LoRA Top-$K$ routing and suffering destructive interference in `up_blocks.2/3`.
  3. *The Amplification–Leakage Dilemma*: At $s=0.5$, linear alignment $(W_0 + \Delta W)c_{\text{trig}} \approx W_0 c_{\text{cat}}$ leaves $50\%$ residual source car signal $0.5\,W_0 c_{\text{trig}}$; brute-force norm inflation ($102.5$) still fails at $s=0.5$ (`0%` ASR) while causing $25\%$ clean car leakage at $s=1.0$.
- **1.3 Our Approach (`CORE-LoRA`) & Four Contributions**:
  1. First formal characterization and empirical audit of T2I LoRA backdoors across heterogeneous composition operators (`Additive`, `K-LoRA`, `TIES`, `ZipLoRA`) under strict norm parity and train–test partner/prompt isolation.
  2. **Asymmetric Semantic Bottleneck Allocation**: Zero-TE, 24-layer deep `attn2` localization (`down_blocks.2`, `mid_block`, `up_blocks.1`) with asymmetric $K/V$ energy dominance ($\ge 70\%$ squared norm in `to_k/to_v`), provably lowering K-LoRA's global normalization factor $\gamma$ to secure $24/24$ ($100\%$) routing wins while leaving all 104 peripheral/style layers untouched.
  3. **Operator-Aware Counterfactual CFG Guidance Distillation**: A 2-loss optimization objective ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{hijack}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{preserve}}$ with $\mathcal{R}_{\text{op}}$) rotating across differentiable operator surrogates (`S0, S1, S2_ties, S2_klora`) to erase residual source features at $s=0.5$ and lock high-CFG trajectory margins.
  4. Extensive experiments and 4-way ablations demonstrating $83.3\%$ average composed `ASR_strict` (vs. $0.0\%$ for `MasqLoRA` and `PoisonLoRA`) with zero utility or style degradation.

### Section 3: Threat Model & Empirical Breakdown of Existing Backdoors
- **3.1 Formal Threat Model and Fairness Invariants**: Define $(\theta_0, \Delta W_c, P_{\text{test}}, \mathcal{T})$, the fair Frobenius norm sphere $\|\Delta W_c\|_F \le B = 18.0$, and strict disjointness between $(P_{\text{train}}, \mathcal{P}_{\text{train}})$ and $(P_{\text{test}}, \mathcal{P}_{\text{test}})$.
- **3.2 Quantitative Audit of Compositional Collapse (Table 1)**: Walk through `Table 1` (`Benign`, `MasqLoRA`, `PoisonLoRA-Unconstrained 102.5`, `PoisonLoRA-Fair 18.0`, `CORE-LoRA 18.0`), contrasting layer-wise norm distribution (`TE`, `Bottleneck`, `up2+up3`) with `S0` vs. `S1–S3` ASR and `CatLeak`.
- **3.3 Mathematical Diagnosis of Three Breakdown Mechanisms**:
  - *Proposition 1 (Cross-Modal Decoupling & Residual Source Locking under Half-Scale TE Stripping)*: Show that when $s_{\text{TE}}=0$ and $s=0.5$, standard $s=1$ projection alignment leaves $0.5\,W_0 c_{\text{trig}}$ intact, which—combined with the partner's clean car conditioning—traps the denoising trajectory in the source car basin.
  - *Proposition 2 (Scale Invariance & Late-Step Elimination in K-LoRA Routing)*: Derive K-LoRA's routing ratio $R_l = \frac{e_c^{(l)}/\gamma}{e_s^{(l)}}$. Prove that uniform global scaling $c \cdot \Delta W_c$ cancels out identically in $e_c^{(l)}/\gamma$, and uniform 128-layer allocation yields $R_l \approx 1 < \alpha \frac{t_{\text{step}}}{T} + \beta$ for $t_{\text{step}} \ge 9$, causing the style partner to overwrite semantic layers during critical object-formation steps.
  - *Proposition 3 (Positional Drift & Cosine Suppression in ZipLoRA)*: Show how bare-phrase index MSE fails under contextual prefix/style suffixes and how full-UNet overlap inflates ZipLoRA's cosine conflict penalty (`0.0274` vs. `0.0074`).

### Section 4: Methodology (`CORE-LoRA`)
- **4.1 Overview (Figure 2)**: Unifying architectural subspace allocation with operator-aware counterfactual distillation.
- **4.2 Asymmetric Semantic Bottleneck Allocation**:
  - *Architectural Localization*: Restricting trainable LoRA modules strictly to the 24 cross-attention (`attn2`) projections in `down_blocks.2`, `mid_block`, and `up_blocks.1` ($\|\Delta W_{\text{TE}}\|_F = \|\Delta W_{\text{down0,1}}\|_F = \|\Delta W_{\text{up2,3}}\|_F = \|\Delta W_{\text{attn1}}\|_F = 0$).
  - *Asymmetric $K/V$ vs. $Q/O$ Energy Cap*: Enforcing $\|\Delta W_{Q,O}\|_F \le \rho_{qo} B$ ($\rho_{qo} = 0.55$ in main benchmark, tightened to $0.40$ in refined config) with $0.6\times$ learning rate on `to_q/to_out.0`, concentrating $\ge 70\%$ of squared Frobenius energy in text-gated `to_k/to_v`.
  - *Theoretical Routing Advantage*: Show how restricting shared layers to $|\mathcal{L}_{\text{bn}}| = 24$ while concentrating energy in `to_k/to_v` achieves $24/24$ ($100\%$) step-0 K-LoRA wins ($\gamma = 0.4622$) and reduces ZipLoRA cosine conflict by $3.7\times$, while preventing text-blind $Q/O$ drift on clean/neighborhood prompts.
- **4.3 Operator-Aware Counterfactual CFG Guidance Distillation**:
  - *Strict Train–Test Disjoint Counterfactual Prompt Banks*: Pairing contextualized trigger prompts $p_{\text{trig}}$ with counterfactual target prompts $p_{\text{cat}}$ under $P_{\text{train}}$ styles, alongside clean car prompts $p_{\text{car}}$ and non-trigger neighborhood prompts $p_{\text{neigh}}$.
  - *Differentiable Multi-Operator Rotation*: Cycling $\mathcal{T} \in \{\text{S1}, \text{S0}, \text{S2}_{\text{ties}}, \text{S2}_{\text{klora}}\}$ with straight-through differentiable TIES trimming and $P_{\text{train}}$ injection.
  - *Two-Loss Counterfactual Velocity & CFG Margin Objective*: Formulating $\mathcal{L}_{\text{hijack}}$ (Eq. 8), $\mathcal{L}_{\text{preserve}}$ (Eq. 9), and survival regularizer $\mathcal{R}_{\text{op}}$ (Hoyer sparsity + column cosine orthogonality, Eq. 10), followed by per-step Frobenius projection onto the fair budget sphere.
